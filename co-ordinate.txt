================================================================================
  3D Visualization of KUET Academic Building and Surrounding Campus
================================================================================

LEGEND:
  Local Coords   = The vertex coordinates defined in the geometry (unit-space)
  Global Coords  = Final world-space position after translation
  T = Translation    S = Scaling    R = Rotation
  Texture        = No textures are used anywhere in this project.
                   All color is passed as a solid glm::vec3 uniform (objectColor).
  Shadow Map     = A single depth-texture (GL_DEPTH_COMPONENT, 2048x2048) is used
                   for shadow rendering - this is NOT a visual texture on objects.

================================================================================
SECTION 1 - PRIMITIVE GEOMETRY (Local / Unit Coordinates)
================================================================================

Primitive        | Local Coordinate Range
-----------------+--------------------------------------------------------------
Cube (vertices[])| X: [-0.5, +0.5]  Y: [-0.5, +0.5]  Z: [-0.5, +0.5]
                 | 6 faces (Back/Front/Left/Right/Bottom/Top), 36 vertices total
                 | Normals: Axis-aligned outward per face
-----------------+--------------------------------------------------------------
Sphere           | Unit sphere: radius=1.0 centered at origin (0,0,0)
(drawSphere)     | 16 stacks x 24 sectors; positions on unit sphere surface
-----------------+--------------------------------------------------------------
Cylinder         | X: [-0.5, +0.5] (width axis)
(drawCylinder)   | YZ: unit circle radius 1.0; Outer wall + 2 circular caps
                 | 24 segments
-----------------+--------------------------------------------------------------
SemiOval         | Half-cylinder (semi-oval) in local XZ: radius 1.0
(drawSemiOval)   | Y: [-0.5, +0.5]; 20 segments, semicircle from -pi/2 to pi/2

================================================================================
